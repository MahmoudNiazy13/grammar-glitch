⚡ Temporal Grammar Field Guide

Cheat sheet for 7th & 8th Grade time-travelers

🔄 Present Simple (Routines, Facts & Habits)

Used for actions that repeat regularly, general truths, or eternal realities.

Rule: Base form verb, but add -s / -es for He / She / It!
"He plays soccer every Friday."
Signal Words: Always, never, often, usually, sometimes, every day/month/week.
Negatives & Questions: Use do / does + base verb (e.g., "She doesn't like broccoli").

📜 Past Simple (Finished Actions in Past)

Used for actions that happened and finished at a definite time in the past.

Regular Verbs: Add -ed (walk → walked, jump → jumped).
Irregular Verbs: They change drastically! (go → went, eat → ate, buy → bought).
Signal Words: Yesterday, last night/year, 2 hours ago, in 1945, when I was 5.
Negatives & Questions: Use did / didn't + base verb (e.g., "We didn't go to school yesterday").

⚠️ Common Student Glitches to Avoid:

  • Double Past Mistake: Don't say "I didn't saw him". Say "I didn't see him".
  • The "He/She" Forgetfulness: Don't forget -s for Present habits: "My dog barks every morning."
  • State Verbs: Facts like "Water boils at 100°C" are ALWAYS Present Simple!
